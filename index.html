Get to know Sundaze Ventures Tour and Travel

About Sundaze Ventures Tour and Travel

Sundaze Ventures is a certified native tour & travel agency operating out of Qazipora Tangmarg, Jammu & Kashmir.

We take pride in offering unmatched hospitality, curated tour packages, comfortable hotel accommodations, and reliable transport solutions to ensure your holiday in Paradise is truly unforgettable.

Our Tour Services

End-to-end travel solutions handled with native expertise

Customized Tour Packages

Cater to various interests and budgets, crafting itineraries encompassing iconic destinations, cultural experiences, and adventure activities.

Accommodation Booking

Secure comfortable and strategically located hotels, houseboats on Dal Lake for a unique experience, or cozy homestays.

Transportation Arrangements

Provide reliable and comfortable transportation throughout the trip, including airport transfers and verified drivers.

Activity Planning & Gondola

Arrange exciting activities like trekking to glaciers, Gondola rides in Gulmarg, white water rafting on the Lidder, and skiing lessons.

Themed & Cultural Tours

Curated itineraries like photography tours capturing Kashmir's stunning landscapes, wildlife tours, or spiritual heritage routes.

24/7 Dedicated Support

Provide a dedicated support line to address any concerns or questions during your trip, ensuring a worry-free experience.
